Exam Instructions

Do's

  • Ensure your face is clearly visible to the webcam at all times.
  • Stay in a quiet, well-lit room where you are completely alone.
  • Keep your eyes focused entirely on the examination screen.
  • Ensure your microphone is active but the environment remains silent.

Don'ts

  • Do not switch tabs, minimize the browser, or open other applications.
  • Do not look away from the screen for extended periods (tracked automatically).
  • Do not talk out loud, read questions aloud, or communicate with anyone.
  • Do not allow any secondary devices (phones, tablets) into the camera frame.
  • Incorrect answers carry negative marks — leave a question unanswered if unsure.
